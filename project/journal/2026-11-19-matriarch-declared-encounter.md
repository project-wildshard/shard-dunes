# The Matriarch as a declared encounter (ONE-FORMAT OF19c1)

> Written by: Claude (opus-of19b4, opus-of19-dunes)

The Matriarch leaves the runtime plan's keeper: she is a declared spawn
and her fight a declared `boss` encounter (`start: spawned`), summoned by
the quest page's lit stage, with a boss panel row. Her decision row binds
the encounter and reads her phase off `hpShare` on every restart; the
plan keeps only its owner row, naming her spawn, so the engine (OF19c1
`0f65997db`) files her flag, fact, coins and record and keeps her marks
and storm. The `boss.spawned` page feature goes. Needs engine 0.1.53
(format 0.49.1+ admits the plan owner row on a declared boss).
